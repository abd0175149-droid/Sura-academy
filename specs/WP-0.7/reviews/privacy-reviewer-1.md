**Result: 0 BLOCKER, 12 MAJOR, 16 MINOR.** No ERD has a child photo, email, phone, address or school column, and no table creates a private adult↔child channel. The big gaps are in how retention and erasure would actually run, free-text fields that are not tagged, and evidence holds for safeguarding.

## Passed
- **PRV-04, ADR-009 §2:** the child's columns are first name, birth month/year, level information and the ONB-03/ONB-04 fields. No photo, email, phone, address or school column exists in any ERD (grep confirmed). Students are not users, and placement is the source of the current level.
- **Consents:** append-only, with `legal_document_version_id` pointing at immutable published versions. Withdrawal is a new row. Recording consent is per child (D-11). Third-party sharing is a separate purpose (PRV-03). Cookie marketing consent is opt-in only.
- **Recording safeguards:** `recording_blocked_reason = missing_consent`. Storage is in eu-central-1, deletion at the provider is tracked, and the 30/90-day clocks come from settings. Families get 10-minute signed links (SEC-10) and every access is logged. Supervisor review requires an incident (CHECK, SAFE-05).
- **No private channel (SAFE-03, CLS-07, STU-10):** chat is scoped to the session with no recipient column, announcements are academy-issued only, support threads are between adults, and homework feedback is visible to the guardian (HW-04).
- **Safeguarding:** the incident table is restricted to the two roles. Its action log is append-only, the officer notification time is tracked, the critical template priority bypasses quiet hours, and retention is 7 years (D-15).
- **Audit split:** the SEC-11 / D-14 split (immutable `audit_logs` with ids and non-personal changes, plus an erasable `audit_log_personal_data`) is sound in principle. Fix the trigger and grants in the findings above.
- **Logging hygiene:** emails and phones are masked in delivery logs, bounced addresses are stored hashed, and outbox and job payloads are ids only. SMS templates never carry child data. Invoice lines carry no child name.
- **Retention coverage:** every row of spec/17's retention table and every D-15 item has a matching row in pii-registry.md. Periods the spec does not state are flagged for legal counsel rather than invented.
- **Child login:** PINs and login codes are hashed, devices are authorized at family level, and child tokens always expire.
