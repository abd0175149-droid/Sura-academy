## Ledger audit: WP-0.7 data-model baseline (docs only)

Scope: the ERD files `ledger.md`, `billing.md`, `teachers.md` (compensation), `platform.md`, `scheduling.md` and `data-model-conventions.md` on `wp/WP-0.7-data-model-baseline`, checked against constitution Art. 5, ADR-007, `state-machines.md` and decisions D-01..D-08, D-28, D-30. I edited no files. `php scripts/check-erd.php` passes (`tables 126 · spec/14 mapped 110/110 · R1 covered 210/210 · erd OK`). Item 8 (tests) does not apply because this WP has no code.

**The unique key, in short:** limiting `(student_id, session_id, type)` to the four session-charge types does honour Art. 5 §2 "لقيود الحصص" and the intent of ADR-007 §6. Leaving REVERSAL outside the key is safe, because `UNIQUE (reverses_entry_id)` keeps each correction chain to a single line. The part that does not honour them is re-posting A→B→A as `ADJUSTMENT_MANUAL`: that puts a session charge outside every uniqueness guard (BLOCKER 1). Separately, the narrowed key conflicts with the written conventions and was never recorded against ADR-007 (MAJOR 3).

### Verified OK
- **Append-only:** `credit_ledger_entries` is append-only with no `updated_at`, the `forbid_mutation` trigger, UPDATE/DELETE revoked from `sura_app`, and rows never deleted on erasure (ledger.md:199). The conventions list (conventions:47) covers ledger, audit, wallet, subscription_events, enrollment_history, consents, setting/rule versions and the logs, and check-erd E4 enforces it.
- **Reversal model:** `reverses_entry_id`, `UNIQUE (reverses_entry_id)` and `CHECK ((type='REVERSAL') = (reverses_entry_id IS NOT NULL))`. Session types must carry a `session_id`, and `EXTENSION_ABSENCE_EXCUSED` must be qty 0.
- **Unique key interpretation:** the four-type partial key itself is a sound reading of Art. 5 §2 (see the summary above and BLOCKER 1).
- **Idempotency keys:** `idempotency_key NOT NULL UNIQUE` on `credit_ledger_entries`, `wallet_entries`, `payments` and `refunds`.
- **`rule_version_id`:** NOT NULL on `credit_ledger_entries`, `credit_lots`, `session_attendance`, `absence_requests` and `compensation_lines`. The `rule_versions` snapshot covers `attendance.*`, `credits.*`, `subscription.*`, `billing.*`, `trial.*` and `teacher.*`.
- **Money:** every `*_minor` column is bigint with a `currency` column. There are no floats or decimals (E3 lint). Settings money is stored as `{amount_minor, currency}` (35 JOD = 35000 fils), and compensation is in fils.
- **No FK cycle:** lots are inserted before entries; migration order is credit_lots #101, credit_ledger_entries #102, wallet_entries #105. `UNIQUE (lot_id) WHERE type='CREDIT_PURCHASE'` allows one purchase per lot.
- **Duplicate payments and webhooks:** `payments UNIQUE (provider, provider_ref)` and `webhook_calls UNIQUE (name, event_ref)` are in place.
- **Subscription states:** `subscriptions.status` lists exactly the 9 states of state-machines.md. There is one live subscription per child, every transition writes `subscription_events`, and the D-07 columns exist.
- **Compensation snapshots:** D-03 `paid_student_count` and D-04 `period_month_on` (Asia/Amman) are snapshotted. `compensation_statements UNIQUE (teacher_id, period_month_on)` and `payouts UNIQUE (statement_id)` prevent a second payout record per statement.
- **Snapshots are cache only (ATT-07):** a daily job compares them with the ledger sum.
- **Trial and paid pools:** `pool ∈ {paid, trial}` is on every entry, and trial seats use `class_enrollments.kind` (D-08).
